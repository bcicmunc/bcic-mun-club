What is MUN?

Model United Nations is a simulation of the real UN. Each student becomes a delegate, researches a country and debates solutions with other delegates. No experience needed.

Speak with confidence

Practise speeches and quick thinking in a friendly group.

Research like a diplomat

Learn how to find facts, read policy and build an argument.

Negotiate and collaborate

Form alliances, compromise and write resolutions together.

Meet new people

Make friends across classes and other institutions.

How a session works

A MUN committee follows seven steps, from preparing at home to the final vote. Pick a step to see what delegates do and how to do it well.

Practise speeches, research countries, debate topics and take part in club events.
